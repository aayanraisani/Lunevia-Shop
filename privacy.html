Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Lunévia collects, why we collect it and the choices you have. By using our website you agree to this policy.

Template note for the site owner: this page is sample text, not legal advice. Replace the contact details, delivery areas, prices and policies with your own, and have it reviewed for the laws that apply to your business. In demo mode this template sends no form data anywhere.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, phone number, delivery address and the items you order.
  • Account details: your name, email, phone and saved address if you create an account. Passwords are stored in a protected form.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact form.
  • Newsletter: your email address if you subscribe.
  • Browser storage: your shopping bag, wishlist and sign-in status are kept in your browser so they are still there when you come back.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and track your orders.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and working well.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. The site owner may share order information with delivery partners when needed to fulfil an order, and with payment providers if online payment is offered. Do not submit payment-card details through the forms on this site.

Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser's storage for essential features: your bag, wishlist and account sign-in. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings, but your bag and wishlist will be emptied and you will be signed out.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email hello@lunevia.com and we will respond as soon as we can. To stop newsletter emails, use the unsubscribe option in the email or contact us.

How long we keep information

We keep order information for as long as we need it to run our business and meet legal and tax requirements. We keep newsletter emails until you unsubscribe.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website can be completely secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

Children

Our website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@lunevia.com or use our contact form.
