Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 2026

This website is a front-end demonstration. Do not enter real personal, account, delivery, or payment information. The demonstration stores sample form entries, account data, cart data, and orders in the current browser and does not send them to a store backend.

Information we collect

  • Demo order details: the name, email, phone number, delivery address and items you enter during checkout, saved locally in this browser.
  • Demo account details: the name, email, phone and address you enter when creating a sample account. Password hashes are stored in this browser; this is not server-side account security.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact form.
  • Newsletter: your email address if you subscribe.
  • Browser storage: your shopping bag, wishlist and sign-in status are kept in your browser so they are still there when you come back.

How the demo uses it

  • To demonstrate cart, wishlist, account, contact, newsletter, and checkout interactions.
  • To show locally saved sample orders and submissions in the demo admin panel when it is opened in the same browser profile.
  • No real order fulfilment, support response, or newsletter email is triggered by this front-end demo.

Sharing your information

This Vercel-hosted front-end demo does not have a connected backend or form-processing provider. Contact messages, newsletter emails, sample account details, and sample orders are stored in local browser storage only; they are not transmitted to the site owner or a database by this code. The same browser profile can expose this data to other scripts running on this site, and clearing browser storage may remove it.

Do not enter real customer information or payment-card details. Before using this project as a real store, connect a properly secured backend or form provider and replace this demo policy with one that accurately describes the deployed services.

Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser's storage for essential features: your bag, wishlist and account sign-in. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings, but your bag and wishlist will be emptied and you will be signed out.

Your choices

You can remove this demo data by clearing this site's local storage in your browser settings. The contact details shown elsewhere on the site are placeholders and must be verified before any real launch.

How long we keep information

Demo records remain in this browser until removed by the demo controls or cleared from browser storage. This code does not send newsletter emails or maintain a central order database.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website can be completely secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

Children

Our website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@lunevia.com or use our contact form.
