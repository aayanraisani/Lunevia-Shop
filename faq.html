Frequently asked questions.

Orders & payment

How do I place an order?

Add the products you like to your bag, open your bag and choose Proceed to Checkout. Fill in your delivery details and place your order. You will see an order number that starts with LV, for example LV1001.

Which payment methods do you accept?

You can pay with cash on delivery. Card payments are coming soon.

Do I need an account to order?

No, you can check out as a guest. An account lets you see your past orders and saves your delivery details for next time.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as you can at hello@lunevia.com with your order number. We can only change or cancel an order before it has shipped.

Shipping & delivery

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is free on orders over $75. For smaller orders there is a flat fee of $5. Your bag always shows the exact amount before you pay.

How long will my order take?

We prepare orders in 1–2 business days. After that, delivery takes about 3–5 business days inside Pakistan and 7–14 business days for other countries.

How can I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter your order number and the email you used at checkout.

Returns & refunds

What is your return policy?

You can return unused, unopened items in their original packaging within 14 days of delivery. Read the full details on our Shipping & Returns page.

Can I return skincare or beauty products I have opened?

For hygiene reasons we cannot accept opened skincare or beauty products, unless they arrived damaged or faulty.

My order arrived damaged or wrong. What should I do?

Email us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@lunevia.com with your order number and a photo. We will make it right with a replacement or a refund.

Contact

How can I reach you?

Use our contact form or email hello@lunevia.com. We are available Mon–Sat, 10am–7pm.

Still need help?

Our team is happy to answer any question.
