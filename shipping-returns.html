Processing and delivery times

  • Processing: we prepare every order in 1–2 business days.
  • Inside Pakistan: about 3–5 business days after processing.
  • Other countries: about 7–14 business days after processing.

These are estimates. Delays can happen during holidays, bad weather or customs checks.

Payment

We currently accept cash on delivery. Please keep the exact amount ready for the courier. Card payments are coming soon.

Tracking your order

Use Track Order with your order number and the email you used at checkout to see where your order is.

Returns and exchanges

We want you to love what you ordered. You can return an item within 14 days of delivery if it is:

  • unused and unopened,
  • in its original packaging, and
  • accompanied by your order number.

Skincare and beauty products that have been opened cannot be returned, for hygiene reasons. This does not apply to items that arrived damaged or faulty.

Damaged or wrong items

If something arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, email hello@lunevia.com within 48 hours of delivery. Include your order number and a clear photo. We will send a replacement or refund you.

How to start a return

  • Email hello@lunevia.com with your order number and the items you want to return.
  • We will reply with the return address and the next steps.
  • Send the items back in their original packaging.

Refunds

After we receive and check your return, we will refund the price of the items to the same method you paid with, or arrange another way if you paid by cash on delivery. Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the return is our mistake.

Questions

Visit our FAQ or contact us. We are available Mon–Sat, 10am–7pm.
